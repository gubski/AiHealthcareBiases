Medical AI inherits the bias it should remove

Why the topic is important
01

Objectivity promised, bias found

Medical AI should remove provider bias, yet models are frequently biased toward certain patient groups. Cross et al. (2024, p. 2)

02

Real harm to real patients

Misdiagnosis and inappropriate treatment — skin-cancer tools trained on lighter skin may miss disease in darker skin. Abu-Mahfouz (2026)

03

Hard to see, hard to fix

Bias compounds through the lifecycle, and weak explainability makes it difficult to uncover and correct. Cross et al. (2024, p. 1); Abu-Mahfouz (2026)

08 · Limitations and challenges

Limitations we accept — and how we respond

Methodological
  • Theoretical only: no algorithm or data is tested
  • Two main sources limit the breadth of evidence
  • Matching bias to solutions needs our own judgement
Our response

A fixed three-step procedure and a framework table defined in advance.

Data and access
  • Published literature favours positive results
  • Secondary sources only, no primary data
  • Some sources may not be readable in full
Our response

Leave such sources out, or mark them clearly as partial.

Ethics and integrity
  • No patient data are involved
  • Every source needs exact citation, with PDF page numbers
  • Many direct quotations can look unoriginal
Our response

Paraphrase more, and always cite the source and page.
